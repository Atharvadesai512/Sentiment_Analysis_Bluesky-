# Assessment preparation

This guide maps the supplied M.A./M.Sc. evaluation scheme to the completed work. Confirm that this is the scheme used by your department.

## Thesis: 140 marks

- Introduction (10): Chapter 1 states the problem, three research questions, objectives and the proposal-to-implementation changes.
- Literature review (30): Chapter 2 compares foundational methods, TweetEval, TimeLMs and existing Bluesky studies; section 2.5 states the bounded research contribution.
- Methodology (30): Chapter 3 specifies datasets, model configurations, metrics, bootstrap assumptions, sampling coverage and timing protocols.
- Results and discussion (40): Chapter 5 presents common-test scores, uncertainty, confusion analysis, temporal sensitivity, inference measurements and queue/storage replay.
- Limitations (5): Section 6.2 identifies domain-label, coverage, pretraining and runtime restrictions.
- Bibliography (10): References supply numbered citations to the sources used. Check any venue-specific reference style before publication.
- Plagiarism (15): No institutional similarity report has been generated. Use the college's prescribed submission/checking process; a score cannot be invented or guaranteed.

## Presentation and viva: 40 marks

The final assessment allocates 20 marks each. Use the 12-slide presentation with speaker notes and rehearse the questions in Viva Preparation.md. Demonstrate the saved outputs and code. Explain why Bluesky agreement is not accuracy and why saved BiLSTM scores do not supply fresh timing.

## Internal assessment: 120 marks

The supplied scheme allocates 30 to the proposal, 50 to progress reports, 20 to presentation and 20 to viva. Use your real proposal, dated work records and actual attendance/logbook entries. This package does not invent retrospective progress or signatures.
